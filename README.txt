Albino Kath Hound Armor
KOTOR 1
Dodrari Kaan

This mod was inspired by the Force-resistant Albino Kath Hound boss. It adds a new armor to the game with Force resistance. Since Kni has a very simplistic dialogue in the game, he is the one who gives it to you.
